Prop. II.4: Pyramids on Triangular & Polygonal Bases
====================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 96
   :page: 100

**Proposition II.4 (Pyramids, Schöne pp. 100–104)**:

By :just:`XII.7`, any pyramid on a triangular or polygonal base equals one-third of the prism with the same base and equal perpendicular height :math:`h`:

.. math::

   V_{\text{pyramid}} = \frac{1}{3} A_{\text{base}} \cdot h.
