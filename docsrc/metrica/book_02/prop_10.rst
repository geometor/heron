Prop. II.10: Alternative Conical Frustum & Slant-Side Reduction
===============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 110
   :page: 118

**Proposition II.10 (Conical Frustum from Slant Side, Schöne pp. 118–120)**:

When a conical frustum (such as a stone column drum or vat) has its slant generator :math:`\ell` measured instead of its perpendicular height :math:`h`, Hero extracts :math:`h = \sqrt{\ell^2 - \left(\frac{D_1 - D_2}{2}\right)^2}` (:just:`I.47`) and completes the volume calculation via Proposition II.9.
