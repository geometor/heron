Prop. II.9: Conical Frustum via Mean-Plus-Third-Difference
==========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 110
   :page: 116

**Proposition II.9 (Circular Conical Frustum, Schöne pp. 116–118)**:

Applying the same identity (:just:`II.6`) to a truncated circular cone (:term:`frustum <kolouros>`) of lower diameter :math:`D_1`, upper diameter :math:`D_2`, and perpendicular height :math:`h` yields:

.. math::

   V_{\text{cone-frustum}} = \frac{\pi}{4} h \left[\left(\frac{D_1 + D_2}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{D_1 - D_2}{2}\right)^2\right] \approx \frac{11}{14} h \left[\left(\frac{D_1 + D_2}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{D_1 - D_2}{2}\right)^2\right].
