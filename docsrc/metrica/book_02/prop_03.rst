Prop. II.3: Hexagonal & Polygonal Prisms
========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 96
   :page: 98

**Proposition II.3 (Polygonal Prisms, Schöne pp. 98–100)**:

Given a right or oblique prism on a triangular, hexagonal, or arbitrary polygonal base, find the base area via Book I (:just:`I.8`, :just:`I.17–25`) and multiply by the perpendicular height :math:`h`: :math:`V_{\text{prism}} = A_{\text{base}} \cdot h`.
