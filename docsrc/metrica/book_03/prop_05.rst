Prop. III.5: Parallel Cut of a Trapezium via Weighted RMS
=========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 148
   :page: 148

**Proposition III.5 (Parallel Cut of a Trapezium, Schöne pp. 148–152)**:

Given parallel bases :math:`a < b`, a cut :math:`x` parallel to the bases divides the trapezium in ratio :math:`m : n` when :math:`x` satisfies the weighted root-mean-square law:

.. math::

   \frac{x^2 - a^2}{b^2 - x^2} = \frac{m}{n} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad x^2 = \frac{n a^2 + m b^2}{m + n}.

Hero proves this by extending the non-parallel legs to meet at an auxiliary apex pyramid-style, reducing the trapezium to nested similar triangles (:just:`VI.19`)!
