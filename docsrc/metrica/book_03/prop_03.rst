Prop. III.3: Dividing a Triangle from an Arbitrary Side Point
=============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 146
   :page: 144

**Proposition III.3 (Division from an Arbitrary Point on a Side, Schöne pp. 144–146)**:

Given a point :math:`\Delta` anywhere on side :math:`AB`, to cut off a fraction :math:`\frac{m}{m+n}` of :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma` by a line :math:`\Delta E`, construct :math:`AE` from the reciprocal proportion :math:`\Delta A \cdot AE = \frac{m}{m+n} BA \cdot A\Gamma` (:just:`VI.15`).
