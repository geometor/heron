Prop. III.22: Dividing a Conical Frustum Parallel to Its Bases
==============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 178
   :page: 180

**Proposition III.22 (Dividing a Conical Frustum, Schöne pp. 180–182)**:

For a circular conical frustum of base diameters :math:`D_1` and :math:`D_2`, the dividing parallel circle diameter :math:`x` satisfies :math:`x^3 = \frac{n D_1^3 + m D_2^3}{m + n}` (:just:`XII.12`), extracted by Proposition III.20.
