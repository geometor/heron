============================================

*Structural Overview of Hero's Axiomatics, Morphogenetic Gnomon, Variational Optics & Geodetic Coordinate Traverse*

1. Three Pillars Beyond the *Metrica*
-------------------------------------

1. **Noetics & Axiomatics (*Definitiones* & *In Euclidem*)**:
   - Pairs Euclidean static definitions with **kinematic generation** (flowing point -> line; compass rotation -> circle; semicircle revolution -> sphere) and **rigid-motion symmetry** (axial rotation of a straight line about fixed poles).

3. **Geodesy & Subterranean Coordinate Geometry (*Dioptra* Ch. 15)**:
   - Introduces a **2D Cartesian orthogonal coordinate traverse** (:math:`\sum \Delta x, \sum \Delta y`) around an impenetrable mountain to reconstruct the hidden right triangle :math:`\triangle BN\Delta` and align exterior sighting triangles :math:`\triangle BO\Xi \sim \triangle BN\Delta \sim \triangle \Delta\Pi P` along a single subterranean line.
