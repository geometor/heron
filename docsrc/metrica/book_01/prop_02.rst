Prop. I.2: Area of a Right-Angled Triangle
==========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 6
   :page: 8

**Proposition I.2 (Right-Angled Triangle, Schöne p. 8)**:

Given a right-angled triangle with legs :math:`3` and :math:`4` (and hypotenuse :math:`5` by :just:`I.47`), completing the oblong rectangle of sides :math:`3` and :math:`4` shows by :just:`I.34` and :just:`I.41` that the triangle is half the rectangle, namely :math:`\frac{1}{2}(3 \times 4) = 6` unit squares.
