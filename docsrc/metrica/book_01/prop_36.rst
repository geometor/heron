Prop. I.36: Lateral Surface of a Right Cylinder
===============================================

.. folio:: Schöne 86
   :page: 84

**Proposition I.36 (Right Cylinder Lateral Surface, Schöne pp. 84–86)**:

Opening the concluding surface-area sequence of Book I (*ἐπιφάνειαι*) via :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` :cite:`On the Sphere and Cylinder <archimedes:sphere-cylinder>`, unrolling the right cylindrical mantle yields a rectangle of width equal to the height :math:`h` and length equal to the base circumference :math:`\pi d`, so:

.. math::

   S_{\text{cyl}} = \pi d h.
