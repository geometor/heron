Prop. I.33: Circular Sector & Chord-Arc Mensuration
===================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 80

**Proposition I.33 (Circular Sector, Schöne p. 80)**:

Having reduced both minor and major circular segments to sectors and triangles, Hero measures any circular sector as the product of half the arc length and the radius (:math:`A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{1}{2} s r`), extending :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` circle-triangle equivalence (:cite:`Measurement of a Circle, Prop. 1 <archimedes:circle>`).
