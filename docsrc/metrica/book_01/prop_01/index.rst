Prop. I.1: Area of an Oblong Rectangle
======================================

.. folio:: Schöne 6
   :page: 6

**Proposition I.1 (Oblong Rectangle, Schöne pp. 6–8)**:

Given an oblong rectangular region :math:`AB\Gamma\Delta` with :math:`AB = 5` feet and :math:`B\Gamma = 3` feet, dividing each side into unit-foot segments partitions the rectangle into :math:`5 \times 3 = 15` orthogonal unit squares. Thus the :term:`area <embadon>` of every rectangle equals the product of two containing sides.
