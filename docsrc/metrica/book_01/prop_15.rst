Prop. I.15: Area of a Rhomboid (Parallelogram)
==============================================

.. folio:: Schöne 32
   :page: 38

**Proposition I.15 (Rhomboid, Schöne pp. 38–42)**:

Given a rhomboid (general parallelogram) with its sides and one diagonal (or perpendicular) specified, the diagonal divides it into two congruent triangles (:just:`I.34`) whose areas are computed directly via Proposition I.8.
