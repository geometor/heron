Prop. I.5: Acute-Angled Scalene Triangle on (13, 14, 15)
========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 10
   :page: 12

**Proposition I.5 (Acute Scalene Triangle, Schöne pp. 12–14)**:

Let :math:`AB = 13`, :math:`B\Gamma = 14`, :math:`A\Gamma = 15`. Since :math:`15^2 = 225 < 13^2 + 14^2 = 365`, the altitude :math:`A\Delta` falls inside :math:`B\Gamma`. By :just:`II.13`, :math:`AB^2 + B\Gamma^2 - A\Gamma^2 = 2 \cdot B\Gamma \cdot B\Delta`, whence :math:`2 \cdot 14 \cdot B\Delta = 169 + 196 - 225 = 140`, so :math:`B\Delta = 5`, :math:`A\Delta = \sqrt{169 - 25} = 12`, and the area is :math:`\frac{1}{2}(14 \times 12) = 84`.
