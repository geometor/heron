Prop. I.31: Circular Segment Strictly Exceeds Four-Thirds Inscribed Triangle
============================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 78

**Proposition I.31 (Archimedean Lower Bound on Circular Segments, Schöne pp. 78–80)**:

Every circular segment strictly exceeds :math:`\frac{4}{3}` of the triangle having the same base :math:`b` and equal height :math:`h` (:math:`A_{\text{seg}} > \frac{2}{3} b h`), as follows by inscribing triangles in the residual segments and applying Proposition I.27 alongside :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` :cite:`Quadrature of the Parabola <archimedes:parabola>`.
