Pappus
======

**Pappus of Alexandria** (*Πάππος ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς*) — fl. c. 320–340 CE
  Preserves extensive testimony to Hero's Mechanics in Synagoge Book VIII and generalizes the torus centroid principle anticipated by Hero's 'unrolled cylinder' proof in Metrica II.13 into the Pappus-Guldin Centroid Theorem (Synagoge VII.42).
  *Loci*: Metrica II.13 (centroid unrolling parallel).
