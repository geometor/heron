Archimedes
==========

**Archimedes of Syracuse** (*Ἀρχιμήδης ὁ Συρακούσιος*) — c. 287–212 BCE
  Hero's supreme mathematical authority throughout Books I and II of the Metrica; Hero cites Measurement of a Circle, On the Sphere and Cylinder, Quadrature of the Parabola, On Conoids and Spheroids, the lost Plinthides and Cylinders, and—uniquely before the 1906 Archimedes Palimpsest discovery—the Mechanical Method (Ἐφοδικόν). Arabic witnesses (al-Bīrūnī) also credit Archimedes with discovering the triangle area theorem proved in Metrica I.8.
  *Loci*: Metrica I.26, I.31–32, I.35, I.37–39; II.11–12, II.14–15 (Method), II.20 (hydrostatic crown/bath displacement).
