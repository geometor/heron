Hipparchus
==========

**Hipparchus of Nicaea** (*Ἵππαρχος ὁ Νικαεύς*) — c. 190–120 BCE
  Pioneer of the treatise On Chords in a Circle (Περὶ τῶν ἐν κύκλῳ εὐθειῶν), the chord-table tradition invoked by Hero in Metrica I.22 and I.24 to compute the regular enneagon (9-gon) and hendecagon (11-gon).
  *Loci*: Metrica I.22, I.24 (Schöne pp. 58–62); Dioptra Ch. 35.
