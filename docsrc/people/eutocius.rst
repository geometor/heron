Eutocius
========

**Eutocius of Ascalon** (*Εὐτόκιος ὁ Ἀσκαλωνίτης*) — c. 480–540 CE
  Attests explicitly in his commentary on Archimedes' Measurement of a Circle that Hero explained the extraction of approximate square roots of non-square numbers in his Metrica.
  *Loci*: Metrica I.8 (square-root iteration testimony).
