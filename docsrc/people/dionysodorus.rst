Dionysodorus
============

**Dionysodorus of Caunos (or Melos)** (*Διονυσόδωρος*) — fl. c. 200 BCE
  Hellenistic geometer whose lost treatise On the Torus (Περὶ τῆς σπείρας) is preserved solely through Hero's citation in Metrica II.13, establishing that the volume of a torus equals a cylinder on the centroid radius scaled by the ratio of the generating circle to half its meridian rectangle (V = 2π²Rr²).
  *Loci*: Metrica II.13 (Schöne pp. 126–130).
