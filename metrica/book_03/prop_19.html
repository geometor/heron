Prop. III.19: Dividing a Polygon Through an Exterior Point¶

Proposition III.19 (Exterior-Point Polygon Division, Schöne p. 172):

Completing the planar division program, Hero locates the bounding wedge from an exterior point \(\Delta\) across a general polygon and constructs the dividing secant via hyperbolic/elliptic application of areas ([III.10], [VI.28]–[VI.29]).
