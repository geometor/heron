Prop. III.23: Dividing a Sphere in a Given Ratio¶

Proposition III.23 (Dividing a Sphere by a Plane, Schöne pp. 182–184; fol. 107v):

Concluding the entire three-book treatise, Hero poses the famous cubic problem of Archimedes' Sphere and Cylinder II.4—to cut a sphere by a plane so that its segments have a given ratio \(m : n\)—and cites Dionysodorus's conic-section solution via the intersection of a parabola and a rectangular hyperbola!
