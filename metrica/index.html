Metrica¶

Μετρικά (Βιβλία Α΄, Β΄, Γ΄) — Hero of Alexandria (ed. Hermann Schöne, 1903)

Author: Hero of Alexandria (fl. c. 60 CE)
Primary Critical Edition: Hermann Schöne (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1903, Codex Constantinopolitanus Palatii Veteris 1, fols. 67r–107v)
English Translation: Original GEOMETOR translation curated for the Digital Scriptorium
Synchronized 4-Track Codex: English · Greek · Summary · Notes

Table of Contents¶

Metrica Book I: Plane Figures, Regular Polygons & Surfaces

Plane geometry of triangles, quadrilaterals, regular polygons from 3-gon to 12-gon, circle and annulus, Archimedean plinthides, parabolic and elliptic segments, and curved surfaces.

Overview · Summary · Notes
Heron of Alexandria

Metrica Book II: Stereometry, Torus, Vaults & Platonic Solids

Stereometry and mensuration of solid bodies, Eudoxus and Archimedes, pyramidal and conical frusta, Dionysodorus’s torus theorem, Archimedes’ Method ungula and groin vault, five Platonic solids, and hydrostatic volume measurement.

Overview · Summary · Notes
Heron of Alexandria

Metrica Book III: Proportional Divisions of Figures

Division of plane and solid figures in given ratios, distributive justice, Euclidean divisions, cevian cuts, exterior-point division via hyperbolic excess, and Hero’s cube root algorithm.
