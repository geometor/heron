Summary — Definitiones, Catoptrica & Dioptra¶

Structural Overview of Hero’s Axiomatics, Morphogenetic Gnomon, Variational Optics & Geodetic Coordinate Traverse

1. Three Pillars Beyond the Metrica¶

  1. Noetics & Axiomatics (*Definitiones* & *In Euclidem*): - Pairs Euclidean static definitions with kinematic generation (flowing point -> line; compass rotation -> circle; semicircle revolution -> sphere) and rigid-motion symmetry (axial rotation of a straight line about fixed poles). - Reduces Euclid’s postulates to three constructive operations and prioritizes direct ostensive proofs over reductio ad absurdum. - Formulates Definition 58 (Universal Gnomon): whatever added to a number or figure makes the whole similar to that to which it was added, realized geometrically by the Golden Rectangle over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

  2. Variational Mathematical Physics (*Catoptrica* Prop. 4): - Deduces the Law of Specular Reflection (\(\theta_i = \theta_r\), or equal spreads \(s_i = s_r\)) from the teleological minimum principle Nature acts along shortest paths (διʼ ἐλαχίστων) by reflecting the target \(\Delta \mapsto K\) across the mirror and applying Euclid’s triangle inequality ([I.20]) to \(\triangle \Gamma ZK\).

  3. Geodesy & Subterranean Coordinate Geometry (*Dioptra* Ch. 15): - Introduces a 2D Cartesian orthogonal coordinate traverse (\(\sum \Delta x, \sum \Delta y\)) around an impenetrable mountain to reconstruct the hidden right triangle \(\triangle BN\Delta\) and align exterior sighting triangles \(\triangle BO\Xi \sim \triangle BN\Delta \sim \triangle \Delta\Pi P\) along a single subterranean line.

2. Exact Symbolic Invariants¶

  • Universal Golden Gnomon (§58):

    \[\frac{Q(A, E)}{Q(A, D)} = \frac{Q(B, C)}{Q(B, E)} = \frac{Q(H, G)}{Q(H, C)} = \phi^2 = \frac{3 + \sqrt{5}}{2}.\]

  • Catoptrica Reflection Inequality (Prop. 4):

    \[|\Gamma E| + |E\Delta| = |\Gamma K| < |\Gamma Z| + |ZK| = |\Gamma Z| + |Z\Delta|.\]

  • Dioptra Tunnel Alignment (Ch. 15):

    \[BN = \sum_i \Delta x_i, \quad N\Delta = \sum_j \Delta y_j, \quad Q(B, \Delta) = BN^2 + N\Delta^2, \quad \frac{BO}{O\Xi} = \frac{\Delta\Pi}{\Pi P} = \frac{BN}{N\Delta}.\]
