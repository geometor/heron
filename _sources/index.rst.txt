===================
Heron of Alexandria
===================

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Heron of Alexandria <hero>` (fl. c. 60 CE), the Alexandrian geometer and engineer whose *Metrica*, *Definitiones*, *Catoptrica*, and *Dioptra* united classical geometry with exact mensuration, geodesy, and variational optics.
