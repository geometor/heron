Prop. II.5: Altitude of a Pyramid from Slant Edges
==================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 96
   :page: 104

**Proposition II.5 (Pyramid Altitude via Slant Edge, Schöne pp. 104–108)**:

When the perpendicular height :math:`h` of a pyramid is not directly observable—only the base sides and the slant edges from the apex to the base vertices are measurable—find the base projection (or circumradius :math:`R_{\text{base}}` when the slant edges are equal) and compute the perpendicular height via :just:`I.47`: :math:`h = \sqrt{\ell^2 - R_{\text{base}}^2}`.
