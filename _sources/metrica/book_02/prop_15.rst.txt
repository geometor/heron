Prop. II.15: Bicylinder Groin Vault via Archimedes' Method
==========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 130
   :page: 131

**Proposition II.15 (Intersection of Two Perpendicular Equal Cylinders — Groin Vault / Steinmetz Solid, Schöne pp. 131–132; fol. 97r)**:

Continuing from Proposition II.14's citation of :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` :term:`Method <ephodikon>` (Prop. 15), when two equal right circular cylinders of diameter :math:`d` intersect at right angles inside a cube of edge :math:`d`—the monumental Roman cross-vault (*καμάρα*)—their common solid is **two-thirds of the cube**:

.. math::

   V_{\text{bicylinder}} = \frac{2}{3} d^3 = \frac{16}{3} r^3.
