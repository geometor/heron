Prop. II.1: Volume of a Right or Oblique Cylinder
=================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 94
   :page: 94

**Proposition II.1 (Right & Oblique Cylinders, Schöne pp. 94–96)**:

Hero computes the volume of any right or oblique cylinder as the product of its circular base area and perpendicular altitude :math:`h`:

.. math::

   V_{\text{cyl}} = A_{\text{base}} \cdot h \approx \frac{11}{14} d^2 h,

demonstrating by parallel shearing (:just:`XI.31`, :just:`XII.11`) that oblique cylinders of equal base and perpendicular height share the same solid content as right cylinders.
