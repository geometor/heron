Prop. II.2: Volume of a Right or Oblique Cone
=============================================

.. folio:: Schöne 94
   :page: 96

**Proposition II.2 (Right & Oblique Cones, Schöne pp. 96–98)**:

Invoking :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>` and :person:`Euclid <euclid>` (:just:`XII.10`), every cone—right or oblique—is one-third of the cylinder having the same base and equal perpendicular height :math:`h`:

.. math::

   V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3} A_{\text{base}} \cdot h \approx \frac{11}{42} d^2 h.
