Prop. II.11: Volume of a Sphere (11/21 d³)
==========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 120
   :page: 120

**Proposition II.11 (Volume of a Sphere, Schöne pp. 120–122)**:

By :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` :cite:`Sphere and Cylinder I.34 <archimedes:sphere-cylinder>`, the sphere is :math:`\frac{2}{3}` of the circumscribing cylinder (:math:`\frac{\pi}{4} d^2 \cdot d`), giving:

.. math::

   V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{\pi}{6} d^3 \approx \frac{11}{21} d^3.
