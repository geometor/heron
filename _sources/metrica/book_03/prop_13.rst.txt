Prop. III.13: Dividing a Trapezium Through an Exterior Point
============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 162
   :page: 168

**Proposition III.13 (Exterior-Point Division of a Trapezium, Schöne pp. 168–169)**:

Extends the exterior-point boundary division method of Propositions III.10–III.12 to a trapezium by completing its non-parallel legs to a triangle or decomposing it into parallel-base subregions.
