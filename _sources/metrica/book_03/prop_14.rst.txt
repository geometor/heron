Prop. III.14: Dividing a Quadrilateral Through an Exterior Point
================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 162
   :page: 169

**Proposition III.14 (Exterior-Point Division of a Quadrilateral, Schöne pp. 169–170)**:

Extends the exterior-point division problem to an arbitrary convex quadrilateral by reducing the target region to an equivalent triangle (:just:`I.37`) and applying Propositions III.10–III.12.
