Prop. III.17: Dividing a Polygon Parallel to a Given Line
=========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 162
   :page: 171

**Proposition III.17 (Parallel Polygon Division, Schöne pp. 171–172)**:

To cut a prescribed fraction from an arbitrary polygon by a line parallel to a given direction, partition the polygon into parallel trapezia and triangles by lines through the vertices parallel to the given direction, locate the bounding strip, and divide it by Proposition III.5.
