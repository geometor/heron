Prop. III.4: Dividing a Trapezium from a Vertex or Base Point
=============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 148
   :page: 146

Extending :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` treatise :cite:`On Divisions of Figures <euclid:divisions>` (*Περὶ διαιρέσεων*) to survey engineering, Hero begins the trapezium and quadrilateral division theorems (Props. III.4–III.19):

**Proposition III.4 (Dividing a Trapezium from a Given Point, Schöne pp. 146–148)**:

To divide a trapezium in a given ratio :math:`m : n` by a straight line drawn through a given vertex or through a given point on one of the parallel bases (cutting the opposite parallel side when possible, or reducing the remainder to a triangle).
