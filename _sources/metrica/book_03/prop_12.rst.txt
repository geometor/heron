Prop. III.12: Dividing a Triangle Through an Exterior Point (III)
=================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 162
   :page: 166

**Proposition III.12 (Exterior-Point Division — Diorismos & Minimum Area, Schöne pp. 166–168)**:

Establishes the exact *diorismos* (condition of possibility) when the exterior point :math:`\Delta` lies in the reflex region, showing which cutting line through :math:`\Delta` achieves the extremal ratio via :just:`VI.27`–:just:`VI.28`.
