Prop. I.33: Circular Sector & Chord-Arc Mensuration
===================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 80

**Proposition I.33 (Circular Sector, Schöne p. 80)**:

To measure any circular sector, multiply half the arc length by the radius of the circle (:math:`A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{1}{2} s r`), following :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` (:cite:`Measurement of a Circle, Prop. 1 <archimedes:circle>`).
