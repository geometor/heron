Prop. I.13: Obtuse-Angled General Trapezium
===========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 32
   :page: 32

**Proposition I.13 (Obtuse General Trapezium, Schöne pp. 32–36)**:

Extending Proposition I.12 to an oblique trapezium having an obtuse base angle, drawing a parallel to one leg reduces the figure to a parallelogram and an obtuse differential triangle whose altitude falls outside its base (:just:`I.6`, :just:`II.12`), again yielding the common perpendicular :math:`h` and :math:`\text{Area} = \frac{a + c}{2} h`.
