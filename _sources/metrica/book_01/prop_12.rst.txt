Prop. I.12: Acute-Angled General Trapezium
==========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 32
   :page: 30

**Proposition I.12 (Acute General Trapezium, Schöne pp. 30–32)**:

Given unequal parallel bases :math:`a > c` and unequal oblique legs :math:`b, d` where the base angles are acute, drawing a line through an upper vertex parallel to one leg (:just:`I.31`) decomposes the trapezium into a parallelogram of base :math:`c` and a scalene differential triangle of sides :math:`(b, d, a - c)`. Applying Hero's Formula (Prop. I.8) or the acute projection rule (Prop. I.5) to that differential triangle determines the common altitude :math:`h`, whence :math:`\text{Area} = \frac{a + c}{2} h`.
