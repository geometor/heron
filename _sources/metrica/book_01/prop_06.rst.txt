Prop. I.6: Obtuse-Angled Scalene Triangle on (13, 11, 20)
=========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 10
   :page: 14

**Proposition I.6 (Obtuse Scalene Triangle, Schöne pp. 14–16)**:

In Hero's numerical example :math:`AB = 13, B\Gamma = 11, A\Gamma = 20`, :math:`20^2 = 400 > 13^2 + 11^2 = 290`, so :math:`\angle B` is obtuse and the foot :math:`\Delta` falls *outside* :math:`B\Gamma` past :math:`B`. By :just:`II.12`, :math:`2 \cdot B\Gamma \cdot B\Delta = 400 - 290 = 110`, whence :math:`B\Delta = 5`, :math:`A\Delta = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12`, and the area is :math:`\frac{1}{2}(11 \times 12) = 66`.
