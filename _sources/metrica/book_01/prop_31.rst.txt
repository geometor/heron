Prop. I.31: Circular Segment Strictly Exceeds Four-Thirds Inscribed Triangle
============================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 78

**Proposition I.31 (Archimedean Lower Bound on Circular Segments, Schöne pp. 78–80)**:

Hero proves that every circular segment strictly exceeds :math:`\frac{4}{3}` of the inscribed triangle of the same base :math:`b` and height :math:`h` (:math:`A_{\text{seg}} > \frac{2}{3} b h`), comparing the inscribed triangle stack with :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` :cite:`Quadrature of the Parabola <archimedes:parabola>` and Proposition I.27 (:math:`\frac{4}{3}` geometric series).
