Prop. I.37: Lateral Surface of a Right Isosceles Cone
=====================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 86
   :page: 86

**Proposition I.37 (Right Isosceles Cone Lateral Surface, Schöne pp. 86–88)**:

By :cite:`Sphere and Cylinder I.14 <archimedes:sphere-cylinder>`, the lateral surface area of a right cone equals the triangle (or circle sector) formed by half the base circumference and the slant side (*πλευρά*) :math:`\ell`:

.. math::

   S_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{2} (\pi d)\,\ell = \pi r \ell.
