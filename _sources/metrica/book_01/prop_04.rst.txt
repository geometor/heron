Prop. I.4: Distinguishing Acute vs. Obtuse Scalene Triangles
============================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 10
   :page: 10

**Proposition I.4 (Acute vs. Obtuse Classification, Schöne pp. 10–12)**:

Given the three sides :math:`a, b, c` of a scalene triangle, before dropping an altitude to side :math:`BC = a`, how do we know whether the angle at :math:`B` is acute, right, or obtuse—that is, whether the altitude falls *inside* or *outside* segment :math:`BC`? Hero invokes :just:`II.13` and :just:`II.12`: compare :math:`AC^2` against :math:`AB^2 + BC^2`. If :math:`AC^2 < AB^2 + BC^2`, :math:`\angle B` is acute; if equal, right (:just:`I.48`); if :math:`AC^2 > AB^2 + BC^2`, :math:`\angle B` is obtuse.
