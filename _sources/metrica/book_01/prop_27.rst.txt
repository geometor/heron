Prop. I.27: Continued Geometric Progression in Ratio 4 : 1
==========================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 66
   :page: 70

**Proposition I.27 (Geometric Progression in Ratio 4 : 1, Schöne pp. 70–72)**:

Let four magnitudes :math:`A, B, C, D` be in continual ratio :math:`4 : 1`; then:

.. math::

   \frac{4}{3} A = A + B + C + D + \frac{1}{3} D \qquad \Longleftrightarrow \qquad \frac{A}{3} = B + C + D + \frac{D}{3}.
