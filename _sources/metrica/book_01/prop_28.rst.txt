Prop. I.28: Ancient Trapezoidal Approximation of a Circular Segment
===================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 72

**Proposition I.28 (Ancient Circular Segment Rule, Schöne pp. 72–74)**:

For a circular segment of base :math:`b` and height :math:`h` less than a semicircle, the ancients measured the segment as :math:`\frac{b + h}{2} \cdot h`, taking the circumference to be three times the diameter (so that for a semicircle :math:`b = 2h` it yields :math:`\frac{3}{2} h^2 = \frac{3}{8} d^2`); yet under the closer ratio :math:`\frac{22}{7}`, this rule falls short.
