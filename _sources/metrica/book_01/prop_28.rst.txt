Prop. I.28: Ancient Trapezoidal Approximation of a Circular Segment
===================================================================

.. folio:: Schöne 74
   :page: 72

**Proposition I.28 (Ancient Circular Segment Rule, Schöne pp. 72–74)**:

For a circular segment of chord (*base*) :math:`b` and sagitta (*height*) :math:`h` less than a semicircle, Hero records the earliest ancient approximation :math:`\frac{b + h}{2} \cdot h`, noting that it matches a semicircle when :math:`b = 2h` with :math:`\pi \approx 3` (:math:`\frac{3}{2} h^2 = \frac{3}{8} d^2`), and explains why it underestimates shallow segments while failing near a semicircle under :math:`\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}`.
