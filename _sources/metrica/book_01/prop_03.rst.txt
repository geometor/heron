Prop. I.3: Area of an Isosceles Triangle
========================================

.. folio:: Schöne 6
   :page: 8

**Proposition I.3 (Isosceles Triangle, Schöne pp. 8–10)**:

Given an isosceles triangle with equal sides :math:`AB = A\Gamma = 10` and base :math:`B\Gamma = 12`, dropping the :term:`altitude <kathetos>` :math:`A\Delta` bisects the base (:math:`B\Delta = 6`), whence by :just:`I.47` :math:`A\Delta^2 = 10^2 - 6^2 = 64`, so :math:`A\Delta = 8` and the area is :math:`6 \times 8 = 48`.
