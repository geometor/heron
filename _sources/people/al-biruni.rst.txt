Al Biruni
=========

**Abū Rayḥān al-Bīrūnī** — 973–1048 CE
  In his Book on the Finding of Chords in the Circle, al-Bīrūnī preserves the medieval Islamic geometric tradition attributing the triangle area rule Δ = √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)) originally to Archimedes, whose earliest surviving Greek proof is Hero's Metrica I.8.
  *Loci*: Metrica I.8; Dioptra Ch. 30.
