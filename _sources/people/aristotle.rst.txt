Aristotle
=========

**Aristotle of Stagira** (*Ἀριστοτέλης ὁ Σταγειρίτης*) — 384–322 BCE
  Formulates proportional distributive justice in Nicomachean Ethics V.3 (echoed in Hero's Metrica III Prooemium) and the teleological principle 'nature does nothing in vain' invoked in Olympiodorus's Commentary on Meteorology III.2 preserving Hero's Catoptrica shortest-path proof.
  *Loci*: Metrica III Preface; Catoptrica Greek Fragment.
