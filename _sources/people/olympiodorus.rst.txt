Olympiodorus
============

**Olympiodorus the Younger** (*Ὀλυμπιόδωρος*) — c. 495–570 CE
  In his Commentary on Aristotle's Meteorology III.2, Olympiodorus preserves verbatim the original Greek text of Hero's variational shortest-path proof of the Law of Reflection from Hero's Catoptrica.
  *Loci*: Catoptrica Greek Fragment (Schmidt pp. 368–372).
