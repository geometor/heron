Schoene Richard
===============

**Richard Schöne** — 1840–1922
  Identified the lost Metrica of Hero of Alexandria in 1896 inside Codex Constantinopolitanus Palatii Veteris 1 in Constantinople and entrusted its editio princeps to his son Hermann Schöne.
  *Loci*: Codex Constantinopolitanus 1 rediscovery (1896).
