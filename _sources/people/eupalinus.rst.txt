Eupalinus
=========

**Eupalinus of Megara** (*Εὐπαλῖνος ὁ Μεγαρεύς*) — fl. c. 530 BCE
  Excavated the 1,036-meter two-portal aqueduct tunnel through Mount Kastro on Samos from both hillsides simultaneously; Hero's Dioptra Ch. 15 preserves the classical Hellenistic geometric solution to the two-portal mountain-tunneling problem.
  *Loci*: Dioptra Ch. 15 (historical engineering archetype).
