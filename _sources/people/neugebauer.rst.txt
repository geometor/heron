Neugebauer
==========

**Otto Neugebauer** — 1899–1990
  Established the definitive chronological anchor for Hero of Alexandria in 1938 by proving that the lunar eclipse observed simultaneously at Alexandria and Rome in Dioptra Ch. 35 occurred on 13 March 62 CE, and traced the Babylonian root-extraction background of Metrica I.8.
  *Loci*: Dioptra Ch. 35 eclipse dating (62 CE); Metrica I.8.
