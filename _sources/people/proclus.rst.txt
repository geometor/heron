Proclus
=======

**Proclus Diadochus** (*Πρόκλος ὁ Διάδοχος*) — 412–485 CE
  Repeatedly cites 'Heron the Mechanician' (Ἥρων ὁ μηχανικός) in his Commentary on Euclid Book I—recording Hero's proofs without reductio ad absurdum, his kinematic constructs, and his Economy of Postulates reducing Euclid's postulates to three.
  *Loci*: Proclus In Euclidem Friedlein pp. 196, 305, 323–326, 340–346, 429.
