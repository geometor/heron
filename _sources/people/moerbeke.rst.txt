Moerbeke
========

**William of Moerbeke** — c. 1215–1286 CE
  Translated Hero's Catoptrica (under the title De speculis, long attributed to Ptolemy) and Archimedes' treatises into Latin at Viterbo in 1269, preserving the full proposition sequence of the Catoptrica alongside Olympiodorus's Greek fragment.
  *Loci*: Catoptrica Prop. 4 (Schmidt pp. 316–365).
