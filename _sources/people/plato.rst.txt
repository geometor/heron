Plato
=====

**Plato** (*Πλάτων*) — 427–347 BCE
  Named by Hero in Metrica II.15–19 as eponym of the Five Platonic Solids (cube, pyramid/tetrahedron, octahedron, icosahedron, dodecahedron) from the Timaeus, and echoed in Metrica III's Prooemium on proportional distributive justice (Laws VI 757b–c).
  *Loci*: Metrica II.15 (Schöne p. 132), III Preface (Schöne p. 140).
