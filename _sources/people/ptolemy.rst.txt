Ptolemy
=======

**Claudius Ptolemy** (*Κλαύδιος Πτολεμαῖος*) — c. 100–170 CE
  Paired with Hero by Olympiodorus as co-witness of the shortest-path reflection demonstration in Optics and Catoptrica, and successor to the chord-table tradition cited in Metrica I.22–24.
  *Loci*: Catoptrica Greek Fragment (Schmidt p. 368).
