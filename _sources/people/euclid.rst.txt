Euclid
======

**Euclid of Alexandria** (*Εὐκλείδης ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς*) — fl. c. 300 BCE
  Provides the deductive backbone of Hero's geometric analyses ('given in magnitude and form' via the Data, angle bisectors and cyclic quadrilaterals in Books III–IV, golden-section pentagon ratios in Book XIII, and area/solid divisions extending Euclid's lost On Divisions of Figures in Metrica Book III).
  *Loci*: Metrica I.5–8, I.18, I.23, II.16–19, III.1–19; Definitiones passim.
