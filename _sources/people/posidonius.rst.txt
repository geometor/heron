Posidonius
==========

**Posidonius of Apamea** (*Ποσειδώνιος ὁ Ἀπαμεύς*) — c. 135–51 BCE
  Source for several kinematic and physical-conceptual supplements in Hero's Definitiones and Proclus's Commentary on Book I, defending Euclidean postulates and classifying quadrilaterals and parallels.
  *Loci*: Definitiones §§1–14, §§50–65.
