Eudoxus
=======

**Eudoxus of Cnidus** (*Εὔδοξος ὁ Κνίδιος*) — c. 390–337 BCE
  Explicitly credited in Hero's Preface to Metrica Book II (together with Archimedes) as demonstrating before Archimedes that every cylinder is triple the cone on the same base and equal height, and every prism triple the pyramid.
  *Loci*: Metrica II Preface (Schöne p. 90–92).
