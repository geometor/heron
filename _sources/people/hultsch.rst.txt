Hultsch
=======

**Friedrich Otto Hultsch** — 1833–1906
  Edited Heronis Alexandrini Geometricorum et Stereometricorum Reliquiae (Berlin: Weidmann, 1864), establishing the first critical Greek edition of the Definitiones and the Byzantine metrological compilations (Geometrica, Stereometrica, Geodaesia) before the rediscovery of the authentic Metrica.
  *Loci*: Definitiones §§1–138 (Hultsch 1864).
