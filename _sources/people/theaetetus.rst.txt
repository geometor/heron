Theaetetus
==========

**Theaetetus of Athens** (*Θεαίτητος ὁ Ἀθηναῖος*) — c. 417–369 BCE
  Architect of the theory of quadratic irrationals (Elements X) and exact edge-to-circumradius constructions of the five regular solids (Elements XIII), which Hero translates into explicit edge-to-volume algorithms in Metrica II.16–19.
  *Loci*: Metrica II.16–19 (Schöne pp. 132–138).
