Heiberg
=======

**Johan Ludvig Heiberg** — 1854–1928
  Edited Teubner Hero Vols. IV–V (Definitiones, Geometrica, Stereometrica, 1912–1914) and discovered the Archimedes Palimpsest in Constantinople in 1906, confirming every quotation from Archimedes' Method (Ἐφοδικόν) preserved in Hero's Metrica I–II.
  *Loci*: Definitiones (Teubner Vol. IV, 1912); Metrica citations of Archimedes' Method.
