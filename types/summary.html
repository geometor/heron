Summary — Definitiones & Universal Gnomon

Noetics & Axiomatics (*Definitiones* & *In Euclidem*): - Pairs Euclidean static definitions with kinematic generation (flowing point -> line; compass rotation -> circle; semicircle revolution -> sphere) and rigid-motion symmetry (axial rotation of a straight line about fixed poles). - Reduces Euclid’s postulates to three constructive operations and prioritizes direct ostensive proofs over reductio ad absurdum. - Formulates Definition 58 (Universal Gnomon): whatever added to a number or figure makes the whole similar to that to which it was added, realized geometrically by the Golden Rectangle over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).
