Construct Hero’s synthetic incircle proof of Metrica I.8 on (13, 14, 15).

Matches Schöne (1903), pp. 20–24 (Fig. 7), setting B = (0, 0), C = (14, 0) (Hero’s \(\Gamma\)), and A = (5, 12) so \(|AB| = 13\), \(|BC| = 14\), \(|CA| = 15\), semiperimeter \(s = 21\), incenter H = (6, 4) (\(X(1)\)), inradius \(r = 4\), extended semiperimeter point G = (-7, 0) (Hero’s \(\Theta\)), orthogonal meet K = (4, 0) on BC, and cyclic-quadrilateral vertex L = (0, -8) (Hero’s \(\Lambda\)).

Construct Hero’s Metrica I.18 Pentagon Golden Section Lemma in \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

Constructs A = (-1, 0), C = (0, 0) (Hero’s \(\Gamma\)), D = (1, 0) (Hero’s \(\Delta\)), cuts AD in extreme and mean ratio via compass so |AG| = |AB| = |BD| = sqrt(5) - 1 = 2/phi, and proves \((|BA| + |AC|)^2 = 5 |AC|^2\).

Construct Hero’s Metrica III.1 & III.2 area divisions on (13, 14, 15).

Constructs both:

  • Prop. III.1: Vertex cevian AV dividing BC at V = (35/4, 0) in 5 : 3.

  • Prop. III.2: Line DE || BC dividing area in ratio m_ratio : n_ratio (default 3 : 1) by constructing the mean proportional |AD|^2 = (m/(m+n))|AB|^2 via a semicircle on diameter AB (Elements VI.13 & VI.19).

Construct Hero’s Universal Gnomon (Definitiones §58) in \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

Starts from unit square < A B C D >, bisects AB at M = (1/2, 0), swings compass arc (M, C) to E = (phi, 0), completes < A E F D > with F = (phi, 1), and subdivides < B E F C > by a second square gnomon < B E G H >, proving recursive similarity < A E F D > ~ < C B E F > ~ < H G F C >.
