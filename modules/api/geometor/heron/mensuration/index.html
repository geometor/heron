geometor.heron.mensuration¶

Exact symbolic mensuration formulas and rational approximations from Hero’s Metrica.

Implements Hero of Alexandria’s plane and solid mensuration algorithms in 100% exact SymPy algebra alongside Norman J. Wildberger’s Rational Trigonometry quadrea identity:

  • Heron’s Formula & Archimedes’ Quadrea (Metrica I.8; Wildberger W.07).

  • Hero’s Iterative Square-Root Extraction (Metrica I.8: \(\sqrt{720}\)).

  • Pentagon Golden Section Lemma (Metrica I.18 Lemma over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\)).

  • Regular Polygon Area Ratios (Metrica I.17–I.25, \(n = 3 \dots 12\)).

  • Dionysodorus’s Torus Theorem (Metrica II.13: \(V = 2\pi^2 R r^2\)).

  • Truncated Pyramid/Cone Mean-Plus-Third-Difference Identity (Metrica II.6–10).

  • Exact Volumes of the Five Platonic Solids (Metrica II.16–19).

geometor.heron.mensuration.pentagon_golden_lemma() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Hero’s Metrica I.18 Golden Section Lemma in \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\).

In right triangle \(AB\Gamma\) with \(\angle\Gamma = 90^\circ\) and \(\angle A = 36^\circ = \frac{2}{5}\cdot 90^\circ\), doubling \(A\Gamma\) to \(A\Delta = 2\cdot A\Gamma\) yields \(\angle AB\Delta = 108^\circ\), so \(A\Delta\) is cut in extreme and mean ratio with greater segment \(AB\): \(AB = A\Delta / \phi = (2/\phi)\cdot A\Gamma = (\sqrt{5} - 1) A\Gamma\), hence \((AB + A\Gamma)^2 = 5 \cdot A\Gamma^2\).

Returns:

Dictionary of exact symbolic invariants and Hero’s \(81 : 16\) rational proxy.

Compute square pyramidal frustum volume via Hero’s Metrica II.6/II.9 decomposition.

Evaluates \(V = h \left[ ((b_1 + b_2)/2)^2 + \frac{1}{3}((b_1 - b_2)/2)^2 \right]\).
