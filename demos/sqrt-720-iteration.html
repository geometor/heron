Iterative Square-Root Extraction on √720 (Metrica I.8)¶

Exact Rational Convergents & Quadratic Error Squaring on Hero’s (7, 8, 9) Triangle — Codex Reference: Metrica Book I (Preface & Props. 1–9): Triangles, Heron’s Formula & √720

Mathematical Derivation¶

On his opening scalene benchmark \((a, b, c) = (7, 8, 9)\) (Schöne 1903, pp. 18–20), Hero obtains \(s = 12\) and \(\Delta^2 = 12 \times 5 \times 4 \times 3 = 720\). Because \(720 = 144 \times 5\) is non-square (\(\sqrt{720} = 12\sqrt{5}\)), Hero introduces the iterative averaging recurrence:

\[x_0 = 27 \quad (27^2 = 729), \qquad x_{k+1} = \frac{1}{2}\!\left(x_k + \frac{N}{x_k}\right).\]

Subtracting \(\sqrt{N}\) and squaring reveals why convergence doubles the number of exact digits at every step:

\[x_{k+1}^2 - N = \frac{(x_k^2 - N)^2}{4 x_k^2}.\]
