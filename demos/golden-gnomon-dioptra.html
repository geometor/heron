Universal Golden Gnomon & Dioptra Mountain Tunnel¶

Self-Similar Accretion in Q(φ) (Definitiones §58) & Subterranean Geodesy (Dioptra Ch. 15) — Codex Reference: Definitiones §58: The Universal Gnomon & Golden Self-Similarity · Dioptra Ch. 15: Tunneling a Mountain from Both Ends simultaneously
