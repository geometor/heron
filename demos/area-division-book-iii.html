Exact Symbolic Verification on (13, 14, 15)¶

On Hero’s canonical estate ABC of total area \(84\):

  • Proposition III.1 (Vertex Cut in 5 : 3): Point V \(= \left(\frac{35}{4}, 0\right)\) partitions \(BC = 14\) into \(BV = 8\frac{3}{4}\) and \(VC = 5\frac{1}{4}\), giving exact sub-areas \(\frac{105}{2} = 52\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{63}{2} = 31\frac{1}{2}\).

  • Proposition III.2 (Parallel Cut in 3 : 1): Marking P at \(\frac{3}{4}\) along AB and erecting perpendicular PR to the semicircle on AB constructs R with exact mean-proportional quadrance:

    \[Q(A, D) = Q(A, R) = \frac{3}{4} \times 13^2 = \frac{507}{4} = 126\frac{3}{4}, \qquad |AD| = \frac{13\sqrt{3}}{2}.\]

    Drawing DE parallel to BC yields \(\text{Area}(\triangle ADE) = 63\) and \(\text{Area}(DECB) = 21\) (\(63 : 21 = 3 : 1\) identically in \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})\)).
